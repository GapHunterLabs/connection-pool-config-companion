Reports a HikariCP setting (spring.datasource.hikari.* or hikari.*, in .properties or YAML) outside its documented safe range: connectionTimeout below 250 ms, maxLifetime below 30000 ms, idleTimeout below 10000 ms, or idleTimeout greater than or equal to maxLifetime.

Outside these ranges the pool does not behave as configured; for example, HikariCP silently ignores idleTimeout when it is not lower than maxLifetime. Both the camelCase and Spring Boot kebab-case property names are recognized.
